#### Intensive Care Unit

People with life threatening injuries or ailments need not only critical care but close and constant attention by highly trained health experts.

[READ MORE](https://www.thelifelinegroupofhospitals.org/intensive-care-unit-2/)

#### Emergency Services

We have modern and fully equipped ambulances to offer emergency services to those in need.

[READ MORE](https://www.thelifelinegroupofhospitals.org/emergency-services-2/)

#### Laboratory Services

We have state of the art laboratories that enable us to carry out various laboratory tests personalized to every patients needs.

[READ MORE](https://www.thelifelinegroupofhospitals.org/laboratory-services-2/)

#### Pharmacy Services

The Lifeline Hospitals has a fully stocked pharmacy to ensure that our patients get their prescriptions.

[READ MORE](https://www.thelifelinegroupofhospitals.org/pharmacy-services-3/)

#### Radiology Services

Health care professionals at The Lifeline Hospitals use them to look for broken bones. problems in your lungs and abdomen. cavities in your teeth and many other problems.

[READ MORE](https://www.thelifelinegroupofhospitals.org/radiology-services-3/)

#### Theaters

Our Hospital has advanced x-ray equipment capable of carrying out various x-ray tests and used by our general practitioners and specialists at the hospital.

[READ MORE](https://www.thelifelinegroupofhospitals.org/radiology-services-3/)

#### Wards

The Lifeline Hospitals provides the best in patient services including meals, general nursing, drugs as part of your inpatient treatment

[READ MORE](https://www.thelifelinegroupofhospitals.org/ward-services-2/)
