# THE LIFELINE GROUP OF HOSPITALS.

The lifeline Group of Hospitals is a health center of excellence which seeks to transform patient care delivery . We are located along Thika Super Highway opposite Kahawa Barracks. The level 5 institution has a multi-disciplinary team committed to delivering the best patient care possible.
